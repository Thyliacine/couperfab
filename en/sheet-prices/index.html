Sheet prices

Sheet-metal prices in Quebec

Draft · first comparison · sources consulted November 26, 2026 (America/Toronto) · to be reviewed by Michaël

Plenty of shops would pay for an honest comparison of steel, stainless, and aluminum sheet. In practice, most Quebec suppliers still say “call for pricing.” CoupeFab does not invent prices: only public lists, dated and linked.

Method

  1. Use only public pages that can be read without an account.
  2. Record the source’s own “last updated” date, and the date we consulted it.
  3. Keep the units as published ($/sq ft or $/lb). Do not convert quietly, and do not mix units in one column without a label.
  4. State clearly that a posted price is indicative, often before processing, quantity, sheet size, and availability.
  5. If only one public source is found for a product, say so: this is a landmark, not a complete market.

This first edition found only one detailed public Quebec list suitable for a table: unitedstainless.ca/live-prices (United Stainless / Alluminox Spec, Saint-Hubert, on Montreal’s South Shore). Other players (service centres, national distributors) did not, at the time of the survey, show a comparable sheet grid in $/sq ft. We did not fill the gaps with shop-floor hearsay or mill index numbers.

United Stainless — indicative list

The page states: “Last updated: October 22, 2026.” United Stainless says it is a custom fabrication shop, that the list is for estimating a project before a quote, and that you cannot order material from the grid. The site’s terms repeat the point: indicative prices only; the final price depends on quantity, availability, processing, and the market.

3003 aluminum plate

Every “Plate … (3003)” row we reviewed (from 0.04 in to 1 in) showed the same price: $4.79/lb. That is a per-pound price, not per square foot.

The same page also listed “Plate 7.048 / 8.82 / 14.1 in (6061)” rows at $28.07 to $56.16/lb. Those descriptions and price levels stand out (shop-grade 6061 usually sits somewhere else entirely). They are left out of the comparison until Michaël confirms whether this is a unit error, a special format, or something else. See the source for the exact wording.

Carbon steel sheet

In the categories visible at the time of the survey, we did not find a comparable public “mild steel plate” grid (in $/sq ft) on that page. The detailed sections reused here are stainless (plate, angle, bar, tube) and aluminum. We will not invent hot-rolled or pickled-and-oiled sheet prices.

Limits of this first edition

  • One detailed public Quebec source. This is not a market index; it is a Saint-Hubert fabrication shop posting indicative costs for fabrication projects.
  • Mixed units ($/sq ft and $/lb): do not average them.
  • No clear minimum quantity, sheet format, or finish (2B, brushed, and so on) for a nesting quote.
  • Angles, bar, and tube on the same page may appear in a later edition, still sourced.
