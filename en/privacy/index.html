Privacy and CASL consent

CoupeFab collects the email you enter, a first name or shop name if you give one, the form language, and the date of consent. For a contact message, we also keep the text of the message. We do not use this data for anything other than sending the newsletter, replying, and keeping a consent record.

Express consent

Newsletter signup requires a checkbox that is not pre-checked. By ticking it, you agree to receive email from CoupeFab (guides, the used-equipment digest, sheet-price updates). You may withdraw that consent at any time, through the unsubscribe link in each issue or by writing to us.

Storage

Signups go through a function hosted on Vercel. When it is configured, they are also forwarded to an operations inbox or a spreadsheet, so early issues can be sent by hand from Gmail. We do not sell the list and we do not share it with advertisers.

Your rights

To review, correct, or delete your email, write to us from the About / contact page. This policy will be updated if a fuller newsletter provider is added later.
